## Deliberate public-access implications

- Calendar event titles, times, calendar names, locations, and Google event
  links are returned by the public read-only events endpoint.
- Google OAuth credentials remain encrypted server-side and are never returned.
- OAuth reconnection remains administrator-only through `ACCESS_CODE`.
- The shared Firestore task and habit data has no visitor identity layer. If its
  Firestore rules permit anonymous writes, every visitor can modify the shared
  task database. Public viewing and owner-only editing cannot be distinguished
  without authentication or a write-specific secret.

## Known timetable constraint conflict

Friday/Saturday QAF (14:30–16:00) and BAO (16:15–17:45) leave only 15 minutes
inside the 16:00–18:00 snacks window. Therefore a 30-minute snack plus 15-minute
buffers on both sides is mathematically impossible. The planner does not create
overlaps and explicitly marks this exception instead of hiding it.
